What You Need for Tteokbokki

What You Need

  • 200g tteok - Korean rice cakes
  • 2 cups water or anchovy broth
  • 2 tbsp gochujang - Korean red pepper paste
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru - Korean red pepper flakes
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 fish cakes (eomuk), sliced
  • 1 boiled egg
  • 1 green onion, chopped
  • Sesame seeds for topping

Steps

  1. Boil water in a pan.
  2. Add gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce and sugar. Mix well.
  3. Add rice cakes and fish cakes.
  4. Warning: stir often - it can stick and burn quickly!
  5. Cook 5-7 minutes until sauce is thick.
  6. Add boiled egg, green onion and sesame seeds on top.
